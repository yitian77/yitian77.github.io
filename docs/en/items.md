# Items and Equipment

BlockZ focuses less on raw item count and more on how each item category affects carrying capacity, recovery, and tactical survival.

## Backpacks

Backpacks are the most obvious source of extra storage, but they are not the only one.

- `backpack_coyote`: a large late-game carrying option.
- `backpack_alice`: classic heavy-capacity backpack for long trips.
- `backpack_czech`: a medium option for progression.
- `backpack_czechpouch`: a smaller early-game or lightweight choice.
- `backpack_patrolpack`: optimized for mobility rather than maximum volume.

## Clothing

Current clothing categories include vests, gloves, tops, pants, and shoes.

- **Vests** usually act as tactical carrying extensions.
- **Tops and pants** often provide extra pocket space.
- **Gloves and shoes** currently support equipment completeness and future expansion.

## Medical Items

Medical resources are a major part of the survival loop.

- `bandage`: core bleeding treatment.
- `rags`: emergency low-tier stopgap.
- `splint`: fracture treatment item.
- `morphine_syringe` and `codeine_pills`: pain relief and condition management.

## Entity and Test Items

The project currently includes `dayz_zombie_spawn_egg` for testing custom zombie behavior and related loot flow.

## System Items

Some items exist mainly to support internal mechanics or special interactions rather than ordinary gameplay progression.

## Extending Item Rules

- Grid size rules can be assigned to third-party items.
- Internal capacity rules can also be extended without hardcoding every mod item into Java logic.
